LINKEDIN
Figma Make lets you iterate on a prototype, but only through Figma's own AI credits, which are limited per seat.

Figmake Sync is a desktop app that pulls the project's code onto your computer and lets you keep going with the Claude Code or ChatGPT Codex subscription you already pay for. No Figma AI credits are spent.

The whole loop in one place: paste your Figma Make link, chat with your own AI (paste screenshots, click any element in the live preview to hand it over with a crop, pick the model, sign in to Claude or ChatGPT inside the app), watch the preview update, follow the agent's work as a step-by-step timeline, review exactly what changed, then send it back to Figma Make.

It is free. Figmake Sync is an unofficial tool by Sherlabs, not affiliated with or endorsed by Figma.

Watch the demo and download: sherlabs.com/figmake
